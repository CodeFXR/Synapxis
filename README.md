<p align="center">
  <strong>Map your Mind!</strong>
  <br><br>
  Synapxis is a fast terminal app for linking notes and visualizing their connections.<br>
  It lets you manage your ideas and view them as a map directly in the console.
</p>

<br>

<h2 align="center">Features</h2>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Rust-Powered Engine</strong><br>
  High-definition Braille rendering for graph visualization.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Neural Graph</strong><br>
  Auto-clusters related notes by color.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Spotlight Mode</strong><br>
  Hover over nodes to dim the noise and focus on connections.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Interactive</strong><br>
  Drag nodes to rearrange your map.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <strong>Wikilinks</strong><br>
  Link notes using standard <code>[[Link]]</code> syntax.
</p>
